# Magical Girl Raising Project
### Welcome to a world of dreams and magic!

**Magical Girl Raising Project** (*魔法少女育成計画, Mahou Shoujo Ikusei Keikaku*) is a Japanese light novel series written by Asari Endou and illustrated by Maruino. An anime television series adaptation by Lerche aired in Japan between October and December 2016. A second season by SynergyP adapting the *Restart* light novel is schedule to premiere in 2026.
